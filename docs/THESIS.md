# Investment Thesis

> Seed/pre-seed AI-agent or agentic-workflow tooling targeting **SMBs, not
> enterprise**, with at least one **technically strong founder**, and
> **visible traction in the last ~12-18 months** (HN traction, a launch in
> that window, or a funding signal).

This is deliberately narrow. A thesis like "good companies" produces a score
that means nothing — this one gives four concrete things to check per
candidate, each traceable to sourced data.

## Scoring rubric (0-100)

| Dimension           | Weight | What raises it                                        | What lowers it                                     |
|---------------------|--------|-------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Team                | 30     | Technical founder, prior relevant exit/experience     | Non-technical or unclear founder background        |
| Product fit for SMB | 25     | Clear SMB buyer, plain-language use case              | Enterprise-only pricing/positioning, unclear buyer |
| Traction            | 25     | HN front-page activity, recent launch, funding signal | Stale (>18mo), no signal found                     |
| Risk (inverse)      | 20     | Few/small open questions                              | Crowded market, unclear moat, key-person risk      |

A candidate outside the thesis (e.g. enterprise SaaS, no agentic component)
should score low on fit even if the company looks strong in isolation —
the thesis is about relevance to this fund, not general company quality.

## Recommendation mapping (guideline, not a hard cutoff)

- **Take a meeting** — strong on Team + Traction, clearly in-thesis
- **Watch** — in-thesis but missing one key signal (e.g. traction too early to tell)
- **Pass** — out of thesis, or a disqualifying risk (e.g. no technical founder)
